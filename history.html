The Santa Barbara Coasters baseball program has grown over time from a ‘22 8U all star team that simply wanted to play more ball, to a full offering of age focused teams and committed families united in a vision to provide a player centric club baseball experience. Each Coasters team is built on a very different model than your traditional club baseball organization. We identify baseball savvy, player-focused, like-minded, and extremely committed managers to lead each of our teams. We then give each manager the flexibility to build a community of players and parents that over time become one, unbreakable, baseball family. Our philosophy will never change and our impact on the community has been undeniable.
