Santa Barbara Coasters baseball is built on the tenets of sportsmanship, passion and pride. True sportsmanship teaches players to respect their teammates, opponents, coaches, and the game itself, showing humility in victory and determination in defeat. Passion drives athletes to work hard every day, pushing through challenges and giving their full effort on and off the field. Pride connects players to their team, community, and personal growth, inspiring them to represent themselves with honor and commitment. Together, sportsmanship, passion, and pride create the foundation of Coasters baseball, shaping not only better athletes but also stronger individuals.
